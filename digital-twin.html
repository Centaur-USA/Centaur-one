Smart Grain Bin Aeration Management

CentaurOne takes grain bin aeration management beyond traditional temperature-based fan controls. By combining real-time grain condition measurements, local weather data, and CFD Digital Twin technology, the system helps determine when aeration is beneficial and when it may cause unnecessary drying or energy consumption. With remote fan control and intelligent recommendations from Chiron AI, farmers can better manage grain temperature, moisture, and storage quality while reducing unnecessary operating costs.
