Understanding Grain Monitoring Technology

What Is a Grain Monitoring System?

Grain monitoring systems help farmers observe stored-grain conditions and recognize changes that may affect quality. Traditional systems often use temperature cables with fixed measurement points. Modern systems may also incorporate moisture sensing, wireless communications, and aeration controls.

How Does Wireless Grain Monitoring Work?

  • Sensors collect grain-condition measurements and transmit data for remote review.
  • CentaurOne uses an outside-mounted, battery-powered Arrow sensor with cellular connectivity.
  • Digital Twin modeling estimates conditions between physical measurements, while Chiron AI analyzes available data to support storage decisions.
