Advanced Grain Monitoring Technology

CentaurOne is an advanced wireless AI grain bin monitoring system combining the externally mounted Arrow sensor, real-time grain moisture, temperature and CO₂ measurements, CFD Digital Twin predictive technology, and Chiron AI grain condition analysis.

YOUR BIN DOESN’T HAVE TO BE EMPTY.

Unlike traditional temperature cable systems, CentaurOne uses a compact wireless Arrow sensor mounted on the outside of the bin that can be installed even when your grain bin is full. Designed for farm bins up to 50,000 bushels, with custom configurations available for larger storage facilities, the system offers simple DIY installation, minimal maintenance, and affordable grain monitoring technology.

One Wireless Arrow Sensor. No Cables Inside the Bin.

Real-time grain moisture, temperature, and CO₂ measurements.

The wireless Arrow sensor mounts on the outside of your grain bin, with a small probe extending into the grain to directly measure actual grain conditions. DIY installation, even when the bin is full.

CentaurOne combines real-time grain moisture, temperature, and CO₂ measurements from the Arrow sensor with advanced CFD Digital Twin technology to estimate conditions throughout the entire grain mass. Farmers can view the actual sensor moisture, temperature, and CO₂ measurements directly on the platform, alongside the modeled grain conditions. Chiron AI analyzes this information to provide predictive insights and help farmers make informed grain management and aeration decisions.
